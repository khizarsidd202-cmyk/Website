Your trusted IT support partner

Apna IT Support provides hands-on technical support, troubleshooting of hardware and software issues, and maintenance of IT infrastructure. We help businesses and individuals resolve IT-related problems efficiently, minimize downtime, and ensure smooth day-to-day operations.

With 10 years of experience in IT support and system administration, we bring practical skills in troubleshooting computer hardware and software, managing user accounts, configuring networks, installing operating systems, setting up CCTV systems and resolving everyday IT issues.

Our goal is to provide reliable, professional and cost-effective IT support tailored to each client's needs — whether on-site or remotely.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do you provide on-site visits?

Yes — on-site support is available across Karachi. For other cities, we help remotely or visit as per availability.

How does remote support work?

With your permission, we connect securely to your computer using a remote tool (like AnyDesk or TeamViewer) and fix the issue while you watch. No visit needed.

Can you install CCTV for my home or shop?

Yes. We do a site survey, suggest the right cameras and DVR/NVR, handle installation and cabling, and set up live viewing on your mobile phone.

Do you repair existing CCTV systems?

Yes — we fix cameras with no video, DVR/NVR issues, cabling faults and mobile app connection problems, and can upgrade old systems.

Do you offer monthly or yearly support for offices?

Yes. Our maintenance plans (AMC) include regular check-ups, updates, backups and priority support. Contact us for a quote based on your office size.

How much do your services cost?

It depends on the job. Send us your issue via the form or WhatsApp and we'll give you a clear price upfront — no hidden charges.
